FAQ

Questions? We've Got Answers

Hover (or tap) on each card to reveal the answer.

How far in advance should I book?

Hover for answer

We recommend booking 6–12 months in advance for peak wedding season (May–October). For off-season dates, 3–4 months is usually plenty.

Do you offer gluten-free or vegan cakes?

Hover for answer

Absolutely! We offer gluten-free, vegan, and nut-free options for every flavor. Just let us know during your consultation.

Can I have a tasting before I decide?

Hover for answer

Yes! Every package includes a complimentary tasting session where you can sample up to 6 flavors. Additional tastings can be arranged.

How much do cakes cost?

Hover for answer

Our packages start at $450 for intimate weddings. Final pricing depends on design complexity, servings, and season. Custom quotes within 24h.

Do you deliver to my venue?

Hover for answer

Yes! We deliver throughout Los Angeles and Southern California. Nationwide shipping available for some items. Delivery is free with Grand & Luxe packages.

Can I customize a package?

Hover for answer

Of course! Every package can be customized to your exact needs. Mix and match services, add extras, or start from scratch — we love a challenge.
