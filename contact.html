White-Glove Logistics

Flawless Venue Delivery &
Day-Of Installation

Transporting a delicate multi-tiered sugar floral centerpiece requires precision and care. We never ship finished cakes via third-party couriers; every delivery is performed by our in-house atelier team in climate-controlled transport vehicles.

✓
Climate-Controlled Refrigerated Transport Guarantees crisp fondant lines and intact Swiss meringue even on summer afternoons.
✓
On-Site Assembly & Florist Placement Our decorators arrive 2 hours prior to guest arrival to perform final tier doweling and sugar flower arrangement.
