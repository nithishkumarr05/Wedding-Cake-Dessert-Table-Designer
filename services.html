Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know

Booking timelines, dietary options, delivery radii, and design consultation details.

How far in advance should we book?

We recommend booking 4 to 8 months in advance for peak wedding season (May through October). We limit our studio to a select number of cakes per weekend to guarantee undivided attention.

Can you accommodate dietary restrictions?

Yes! We offer dedicated gluten-free, vegan, nut-free, and dairy-free tiers, as well as specialty individual dessert shooters so every guest is accommodated safely without compromising taste.

How does delivery and venue coordination work?

Our team contacts your venue coordinator 2 weeks prior to confirm arrival windows, kitchen storage, and staging tables. We deliver via climate-controlled transport and complete on-site floral assembly.

Do you supply cake stands and dessert risers?

Yes, our full dessert table service and luxe packages include complimentary stand and pedestal rentals (vintage brass, polished marble, frosted glass, and ceramic platters) with easy post-event return.
