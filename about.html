Our Journey

A Kitchen Dream That Rise Into a Studio

Sweet Serenity began in 2015 in a tiny Los Angeles kitchen with just one oven, a stand mixer, and a passion for making moments unforgettable. Our founder, Isabella Hart, had one simple belief: every love story deserves a cake as unique as the couple.

What started as a favor for a friend's wedding quickly turned into a word-of-mouth phenomenon. Within two years, Sweet Serenity had opened its first studio, expanded to a team of ten artisans, and shipped cakes across the country.

Today, we've crafted over 500 wedding cakes, each one a hand-designed centerpiece that tells a story. But no matter how big we grow, that same tiny-kitchen feeling remains — personal, passionate, and made with love.
