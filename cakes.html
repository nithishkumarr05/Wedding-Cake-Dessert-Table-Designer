The Design Codex

Architectural Confectionery as an Art Form

Our signature collection is born at the intersection of haute couture fashion and architectural balance. Rather than adhering to standard templates, each cake tier is conceived with deliberate proportion, calculating height-to-diameter ratios that create a majestic silhouette within high-ceiling venues or intimate garden settings.

From custom-tempered Swiss meringue velvet finishes to stone-textured bas-relief carvings, every surface treatment interacts dynamically with natural ceremony light, providing a radiant centerpiece that commands admiration from arrival to the first cut.
