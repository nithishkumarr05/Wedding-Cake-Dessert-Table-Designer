Our Journey

A Kitchen Dream That Rise Into a Studio

Sweet Serenity began in 2015 in a tiny Los Angeles kitchen with just one oven, a stand mixer, and a passion for making moments unforgettable. Our founder, Isabella Hart, had one simple belief: every love story deserves a cake as unique as the couple.

Today, we've crafted over 500 wedding cakes, each one a hand-designed centerpiece that tells a story.

Questions? We've Got Answers

We recommend 6–12 months in advance for peak season.

Absolutely! We offer gluten-free, vegan, and nut-free options.

Yes! Every package includes a complimentary tasting session.

Packages start at $450. Final pricing depends on complexity.
